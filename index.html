Features

Every mode uses the same core logic, tuned to a different game event.

Dynamic Volume Control

Music automatically ducks during important gameplay moments, then smoothly returns when the danger is over.

Walking

Volume lowers whenever you hold your Walk key (e.g. Shift), so you can actually hear what's around you.

Clutch Mode

Volume drops automatically when you're the last player alive — full focus for your 1vX situations.

Death Mode

Full volume comes back the moment you die. Spectate your teammates without touching the pause button.

Round & Buy Phase

Separate volume levels for active combat versus shopping time, detected automatically each round.

App Mixer

Choose exactly which apps get controlled — mute Spotify while Discord stays at normal volume.

How to use

ZoneSync uses the Overwolf Game Events Provider to detect game states locally on your machine.

  1. 01

    Install

    Get ZoneSync from the Overwolf Store.

  2. 02

    Launch Valorant

    The app detects the game automatically.

  3. 03

    Configure

    Set your preferred volume levels for each game state.

  4. 04

    Play!

    ZoneSync runs quietly in the background.
